## Positioning Notes

The product intentionally avoids:
- fear-based messaging
- aggressive cost-cutting language
- “replace your team” AI framing

The tone should feel:
- operational
- helpful
- trustworthy

---

## CTA Philosophy

The product should feel:
- lightweight
- low-friction
- fast to evaluate

Avoid:
- “Book a demo”
- enterprise sales-heavy flows
- long onboarding funnels

---

- “AI tooling costs add up faster than most startups expect.”
