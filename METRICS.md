# Product Metrics Notes

## Core Product Questions

The primary goal of the MVP is validating whether engineering teams care enough about AI spend visibility to repeatedly use lightweight audit tooling.

## Key Metrics

### Audit Completion Rate

Measures:
- onboarding clarity
- form usability
- perceived value

### Share Rate

Tracks:
- how often users generate public audit links
- whether reports are useful enough to forward internally

### Repeat Usage

Indicates:
- ongoing spend monitoring interest
- operational usefulness

### Average Estimated Savings

Useful for understanding:
- whether optimization opportunities are meaningful
- whether recommendations feel credible

## Metrics Intentionally Avoided

The MVP intentionally avoids over-tracking:
- time-on-site
- excessive analytics events
- vanity engagement metrics

## Qualitative Signals

More important early signals:
- users sharing reports internally
- requests for integrations
- requests for historical tracking
- teams returning after budget reviews

## Product Learning Goals

Key things worth validating:
- which AI tools overlap most frequently
- whether startups prefer lightweight workflows over enterprise procurement systems
- how teams think about AI budgeting operationally
