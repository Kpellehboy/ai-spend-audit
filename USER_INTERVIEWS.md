# User Interview Notes

## Current Status

I did not conduct formal user interviews during the assignment timeline.

Instead, I prepared a lightweight customer discovery framework to validate the product direction with startup founders and engineering managers.

---

## Assumptions Being Explored

The current MVP is based on several assumptions:

1. AI tooling adoption is becoming fragmented across startups.
2. Engineering teams often use overlapping AI assistants.
3. Smaller teams lack centralized visibility into AI spend.
4. Lightweight reporting may be more appealing than enterprise procurement tooling.

These assumptions still require validation through real conversations.

---

## Proposed Interview Questions

### For Engineering Managers

- How does your team currently manage AI tooling spend?
- Do you know which AI subscriptions overlap today?
- Have unused AI seats become a problem?
- How do you evaluate whether AI tooling is cost effective?

### For Founders

- Has AI tooling spend grown faster than expected?
- Which tools are now standard across your team?
- Would lightweight audit reporting help budgeting discussions?
- What would make this product genuinely useful?

---

## What I Would Look For

Key signals:
- repeated concern around overlapping subscriptions
- difficulty tracking seat ownership
- requests for integrations
- interest in shared reporting workflows

---

## Important Constraint

I intentionally avoided fabricating interviews or fake metrics.

The goal of this document is to outline how real product validation would happen after the MVP stage.
